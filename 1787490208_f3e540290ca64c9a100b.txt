MYSQL TASK - EMPLOYEE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

1. Create a database named company_db.

2. Create an employees table with employee_id, employee_name, email, department, salary, city, experience, and status. Make employee_id the PRIMARY KEY.

3. Make employee_name, email, and department NOT NULL.

4. Insert at least 10 employee records into the employees table.

5. Display all employee records.

6. Display only employee_name, department, and salary.

7. Display employees whose salary is greater than 50000.

8. Display employees who have more than 2 years of experience.

9. Display employees who work in the Java department AND have a salary greater than 50000.

10. Display employees who are from Pune OR Mumbai.

11. Display employees who work in the Java OR Python department.

12. Display employees who are NOT from Pune.

13. Display employees whose salary is greater than 40000 AND experience is greater than 1.

14. Display employees whose city is Pune OR Mumbai AND salary is greater than 40000.

15. An employee gets a salary increment. Update the salary of a particular employee.

16. An employee moves from Pune to Mumbai. Update the employee's city.

17. An employee leaves the company. Delete that employee's record.

18. Add a new column phone to the employees table using ALTER TABLE.

19. Remove the phone column using ALTER TABLE.

20. Create a temporary table named old_employees and then DROP the table.
